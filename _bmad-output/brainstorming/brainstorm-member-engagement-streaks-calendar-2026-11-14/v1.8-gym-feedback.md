## F1. Pay-per-session validity (per-gym setting)

Need: some gyms (Martin Fitness) want each paid session to be good for one entry; others keep today's "paid once, valid the whole gym-local day".

Recommendation:
- New per-gym setting next to "Charge guests per session" (FR-157): **Session validity: Whole day (default, today's behaviour) | Single use**. Owner/Supervisor, audit-logged with old and new value, like FR-157.
- Whole day: nothing changes. Existing gyms stay on it.
- Single use: every check-in that starts a visit consumes one session; leaving and coming back later the same day costs another (the Martin Fitness morning/evening case).
- Applies to new check-ins only; sessions already consumed keep their covered day.

Decided (owner, 2026-11-15, "go with your recommendations"):
- "One use" = **one visit** (check-in to check-out), so an accidental double scan while still inside is not charged twice (FR-044 already allows one open check-in at a time).
- Defaults carried with it, for the PRD to confirm: a class attendance in a single-use gym consumes its own session unless the member is already checked in and inside (FR-174 today: one session covers floor and class that day); loyalty (FR-165) stays as is, one guest stamp per consumed session, so single-use gyms earn stamps faster and owners tune N.

## F2. Annual bundles: how does it work today

Answer to give the gym (verified in schema and plan form): a plan has a **billing interval (monthly or annual)**, a **price**, a **duration in days**, and for annual plans an optional **gym-set discount percent**. So an annual bundle is a plan with an annual interval and a duration of about 365 days. The member pays once, in full, and the subscription runs for the plan's duration. The normal lifecycle applies: reminder 7 days before expiry, then grace period, then expired (FR-027 to FR-030). Renewal is a new payment for the next year (FR-032). A member who wants to pay monthly takes a monthly plan; the two are separate plans, not two ways of paying the same plan.

Decided (owner, 2026-11-15): **no instalments in V1.8**. Not supported today; note as a later request and see whether other gyms ask. The dashboard could make the annual case clearer with plain wording on the plan card (price, "valid 365 days, paid once").

## F3. A bundle assigned by mistake

Finding: staff can assign a plan when creating a member or later via the initial-assignment action, and the member edit screen shows the plan as read-only. No action to correct or swap a wrong plan was found. Only the registration fee has a "recorded in error" void (FR-151), and refunds exist for payments (FR-040).

Recommendation: a **Correct plan** action for Owner, Supervisor and Manager on a member's current subscription, modelled on FR-151:
- Mandatory reason, audit-logged with old plan, new plan and old and new expiry.
- No payment yet recorded for that subscription: swap the plan and recompute expiry from the original start date. Clear and cheap.
- A payment already recorded: the correction must say what happens to the money, using the existing refund path (FR-040) then assign the right plan. Recommended: refuse the swap until the payment is refunded or voided, so books stay honest.
- Consumed sessions, loyalty stamps and attendance history are never rewritten.

Decided (owner, 2026-11-15): the wrong-bundle case is **mainly before payment**, so the direct swap (no payment yet recorded) is the main flow and ships first. The after-payment case stays as the safe fallback: the swap is refused until that payment is refunded or voided, so no new money logic is needed.

## F4. Classes

**F4a. Different time on each day.** Today a recurring class has a set of days and one time for all of them. Recommended: a recurring class holds a **list of day-and-time slots** (for example Monday 07:00, Wednesday 18:30, Saturday 09:00), each slot can also have its own length if the class has one. Existing recurring classes migrate to the same time on every selected day, so nothing changes for them. Booking, capacity, reminders (FR-116) and the 2-hour cancellation cutoff keep working per generated session.

**F4b. Owner, Supervisor and Manager see what the Coach sees.** Today the Coach Portal (FR-053, FR-122) is for coaches only. Recommended: those three roles can open the same coach views (a coach's classes, class rosters, assigned members and their workout plans and progress) for **any** coach in the gym, read-only unless they already have rights on that data. Decided (owner, 2026-11-15, recommendation accepted): a **coach picker inside the Coach Portal**, because it reuses the existing screens. Keep member privacy rules (FR-095) intact; staff above the coach already have gym-wide member access.

**F4c. Coach can create classes.** Amends FR-104 (Manager or Owner). Recommended: a Coach can create, edit and cancel **their own** classes (coach is fixed to themselves); Manager and above keep full control of all classes.

## F5. Staff check-in for a member who cannot use their phone

Finding: members check in only by scanning the gym QR from their own app (FR-042). No staff action to check a member in was found; staff can only mark class attendance and check people out.

Recommendation: on the Attendance page, a **Check in a member** action (Receptionist and above): search member, confirm, done.
- Creates an ordinary attendance record marked as **manual, entered by [staff name]**, audit-logged.
- Same member-status rules as a scan: expired members are rejected with the red alert (FR-031, FR-049); expiring and grace members are accepted with the yellow alert. Pay-per-session at a charging gym consumes or requires a session exactly as a scan does (FR-159, or the F1 rule).
- Counts toward occupancy and, for V1.8, toward streaks and the leaderboard, because those read attendance records. A member without a smartphone will not see them; that is fine.
- Check-out already works from the desk (FR-045).

Decided (owner, 2026-11-15): every member keeps a required phone number (FR-020 unchanged). The case is a member who cannot use their phone at the entrance (no smartphone, dead battery, app problem); a staff member checks them in at the entrance. No change to member creation, invites or OTP.
