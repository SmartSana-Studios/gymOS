# PRD Quality Review — GymOS, V1.8 layer only

Scope: Section 1.4, last three rows of 3.2, 4.6, UJ-15..17, 6.33-6.41 (FR-219..FR-250), 7.9 (NFR-027..029), new Section 8 rows, OQ-32..35, 10.3, ten new Glossary rows, Section 13. Existing text was read only as context (every cited FR was opened and compared against its citation). Rubric: `.claude/skills/bmad-prd/assets/prd-validation-checklist.md`.

## Overall verdict

The V1.8 layer is well organized and honest about its stakes. Decisions are stated as decisions, "simplicity" is a real guiding constraint, the Section 8 non-goals do real work, FR ranges are contiguous and agree across 1.4, 4.6, 6.33-6.40, 10.3 and 13, and most cross-references resolve and say what they claim. Where it falls short is done-ness. The engagement rules (Shield, comeback, offline day attribution, tie order, back-fill for existing members) and the Single-use and Correct-plan rules each leave at least one point that two developers would build differently, and a few conflict outright with existing text (FR-061, FR-179, FR-154, 4.1 "own data only"). The leaderboard default exposes member names before the member has seen the consent notice. Section 10.3 does not cover every FR.

Grade under the validate.md scale: **Fair** (any high finding, one thin dimension). No critical findings. 7 high, 13 numbered medium and 10 numbered low, plus the unnumbered cross-reference and 10.3 coverage items (roughly 6 more at medium).

## Decision-readiness — adequate

Strengths: the product owner's 2026-11-15 decisions are listed as decisions (Section 13); the "no anti-cheat" call is paired with its condition ("holds only while stakes stay low: rank carries no prize"); OQ-34 names a real tension (FR-095 vs the request) and does not smooth it over; the Must/Should/Could table is honest about what can slip. Weaknesses: OQ-35 bundles seven assumptions into one "question" with no owner-per-item and no ordering. OQ-33 is not a blocker for anything ("post-release check"). OQ-32 recommends an answer and still treats the privacy risk as only "have counsel look if required", although the proposed default changes what every existing member exposes (see H1).

### Findings
- **high** H1: Leaderboard default exposes members before they have seen the consent notice (FR-227, OQ-32, 4.1, 4.6). FR-227 makes every member visible (first name + last initial) by default. The notice appears "the first time a member opens the leaderboard". A member who never opens it, which is every existing member on the day of release, is ranked and named to the whole gym with no notice. This is the first member-to-member data flow in the product, yet 4.1 still says Member = "own data only" and 4.6 does not amend it. The leaderboard also reveals visit counts ("how many visits behind the one above"). There is no gym-level off switch for the Owner either. *Fix:* either hidden-until-first-open (rank counted, name shown only after the member has seen the notice and not hidden), or state explicitly that exposure-before-notice is accepted; add an amendment line to 4.1 for the leaderboard; decide whether the Owner gets an off switch.
- **medium** M1: 1.4 says "Existing gyms are not changed silently" and lists two defaults, but FR-233 (N-08 on by default for every existing member of every existing gym) and FR-227 (visible by default) are the two defaults that change what existing members experience at release. The principle is stated for gym settings only. *Fix:* extend the sentence to say what is silent for members and why that is accepted, or make N-08 opt-in for existing members.
- **low** L1: OQ-33 "Blocker for: Post-release check" is not an open question gating work. It is a monitoring note. OQ-35 reads as an assumptions list (it duplicates the Section 13 index). *Fix:* keep OQ-35 as a pointer only, or split per item with an owner and stage.
- **low** L2: 10.3 says steps run "in the order of Section 13", but Section 13 labels that order `[ASSUMPTION — proposed ... confirm at epic planning]`. The gate depends on an unconfirmed assumption. *Fix:* confirm the order or drop the binding sentence from 10.3.

NFRs carry product-specific tests (server-side leaderboard privacy per read path, determinism under offline sync, one reminder per member per day, safe to run twice). Personas are named and each UJ drives a decision. No innovation or vision theater.

- **low** L3: NFR-029's "gym at its tier's member cap" is not computable for Elite (">100", no cap, OQ-1) or for the configurable Free/Test tier (FR-139). *Fix:* give a concrete member count to test at (for example 300 members), flagged as an assumption.

As written it passes with a normal i18n file (which FR-014-FR-018 already require), so it adds nothing testable. *Fix:* state the intended editor and one observable test, or fold it into FR-014.

## Done-ness clarity — thin

The FRs mostly have observable consequences, and 10.3 is a real gate. The shortfalls are concentrated in the behaviors with state over time. Each item below is something two developers would implement differently.

- **high** H2: Offline day attribution contradicts FR-061 and NFR-028 (FR-225, NFR-028, 10.3 Step 3.7). FR-225 says an offline scan older than the auto-timeout window "is a new visit at receipt time" (borrowing FR-159). FR-159's rule applies only while "Charge guests per session" is on and the member is Pay-per-session. FR-061 says that for everyone else a late sync is **accepted as the scan time**. So for ordinary members FR-225 changes the existing check-in rule, while the same FR and 10.3 3.7 promise "no check-in rule changed". NFR-028 then says a late offline visit "corrects the counts it belongs to", which is the opposite of re-dating it to receipt time. *Fix:* choose one rule for attribution of a late scan to a visit day (scan time when within the window, receipt time otherwise) and say it applies to engagement counts only, not to attendance; restate NFR-028 to match; add the test to 10.3.
- **high** H3: No start state for existing members (FR-219, FR-220, FR-222, FR-226, FR-229, FR-230, NFR-028). Existing members have months of attendance. The PRD does not say whether streaks, best streak, Shields, badges and milestones are computed back over history at release or start from zero. A member with 45 visit days: does the 7th and 30th celebration fire on first launch, are badges granted silently, does the weekly streak count weeks before the goal existed ("the goal in force that week" has no history before release)? The "derived from attendance" statement (FR-225, NFR-028) cannot be literally true either, because the weekly goal (and its history), Shield ledger, badges, anonymous flag and "milestone shown once" are new stored state. *Fix:* one paragraph in 6.33: "history is [counted from / not counted before] release", the default goal assumed for past weeks, and a rule for "shown once" (per member, server-stored).
- **high** H4: Single-use semantics are incomplete and FR-239 contradicts itself (FR-237, FR-238, FR-239, FR-044, FR-045, UJ-16). (a) FR-239 says a change "applies only to visits that start after it" and also that "sessions already consumed keep their coverage". Switching Whole day to Single use at 14:00, a member consumed a session at 10:00 under Whole day, so a visit starting at 15:00 is both "after the change" (needs a new session) and "covered by an already-consumed session". The reverse switch has the same hole. (b) In Single use a visit ends only by check-out or auto-timeout (FR-045, default 8 h). A member who leaves without checking out and returns within 8 h is rejected "already checked in" at no cost, so in effect the gym gets neither a new session nor a second charge. This is the leak V1.6 was meant to close. The only recourse is staff check-out. FR-044's stale open check-in edge case (auto-close then record the new check-in) is not mentioned: does it consume a session? UJ-16's example works only because 7 am to 6 pm exceeds 8 h. (c) FR-238: when a class attendance consumes a session with no open check-in, does it cover a later floor check-in (FR-174 says "and the reverse")? The staff mark attendance after the class, when the member may already have checked out. *Fix:* state the precedence in FR-239; state how a member re-enters in Single use (self check-out prompt, or accept the leak as a known limit in Section 8); amend UJ-16 to show the short-gap case; specify the class-then-floor and floor-then-class cases.
- **high** H5: Correct-plan guards use undefined payment states (FR-242, FR-243, UJ-17, 10.3 Step 1.3). "No payment ... stands as paid" is not defined. Not covered: a manual payment sitting in the verification queue (FR-037); a Tara Money collection in flight (the correction proceeds, then the webhook confirms a payment for the old plan's price); a partial refund (FR-162 treats partial refunds differently from full ones; does the payment still "stand"?); a CSV-imported member (FR-154) whose subscription has no payment record at all, so the guard always passes; a payment-less renewal override (FR-032), after which "original start date" is ambiguous (first start or start of the current period, FR-050 resets the start date at renewal). FR-243 also does not say what a correction to or from Pay-per-session does to expiry (that plan type has none, FR-024), nor whether the recomputed status (expired, expiring_soon) triggers N-01..N-03 and the front-desk alert. *Fix:* list the payment states that block (pending, verified, in-flight) and those that do not (refunded in full, voided, failed); say "start date of the current subscription record"; state the notification/alert behavior of a correction that lands in `expiring_soon` or `expired`.
- **high** H6: Streak, Shield and comeback rules are ambiguous (FR-220, FR-221, FR-222, FR-223, FR-224, FR-233, NFR-028, UJ-15).
  - Does a Shield-saved week add 1 to the weekly streak or only preserve it? If it adds 1, can a saved week help reach the next multiple of 4 and earn a further Shield?
  - One Shield pool serves both streaks. A missed single day inside a week whose goal can still be met (FR-221 "a missed day can be saved") consumes a Shield automatically, so the member cannot keep it for a missed week.
  - The reminder and the at-risk cell fire when a streak "would end today". Does a held Shield suppress them (a visit is not needed) or not (the Shield would be spent)? Not stated.
  - A late offline visit (NFR-028) that falls in a week already closed and saved by a Shield: is the Shield returned? "A Shield cannot be used twice for the same period" does not answer it.
  - FR-223: "restores the member's active streak status" and "the new streak counts from the first week the goal is met again" describe two different outcomes (restore vs restart). The 10-day window has no start (end of the broken week? first visit after?). The comeback state has no end if never completed.
  - FR-219: a member who starts on Thursday faces a goal of 3 in 4 days (first partial week) with no rule.
  - UJ-15 says "on Thursday the ring closes and her weekly streak reads '5 weeks'", but FR-220 says the streak is computed on the server **when a week closes**. UJ-15's sequence in week six (a Shield saves the missed week, and also on "the following Saturday" a reminder fires and "the ring closes") conflates the saved week with the next week.
  - *Fix:* add a worked table of 6 to 8 weeks of attendance with the expected streak, Shield and comeback state per week; correct UJ-15 to match.
- **high** H7: Release gate (10.3) misses FRs; see "10.3 coverage" below.
- **medium** M4: Leaderboard ordering and population are under-specified (FR-226, FR-227). "Ties share a rank and are ordered by who reached the count first": a shared rank and an ordering are two things, and "reached the count first" needs a timestamp definition (first check-in of the Nth visit day? the sync time?). A late offline sync reorders it, and a staff check-in has its own time. "Top 10" with a tie at tenth place: show 10 rows or 10 ranks? "How many visits behind the one above": above means the next higher rank, or the row above in an ordered tie? Members with no visits are "not ranked", so what does the member's own row show to them? Deactivated members (FR-083), awaiting-fee members and expired members: ranked, named, or excluded? Staff accounts that are also members? *Fix:* define the sort key and the population; one worked example.
- **medium** M5: Class-slot edits with bookings (FR-245, 10.3 Step 2.3). "Applies to future sessions that have no bookings; a future session that has bookings is left as it is and is listed to the editor, who decides what to do with it." Undecided: what the editor can do (nothing in FR-104..FR-108 cancels a booked session or notifies members); whether the new-time session is also created on that day, which would give two sessions the same day when Monday 07:00 moves to 08:00; what "future" means (sessions are generated to what horizon? unspecified); whether a removed slot's unbooked sessions are deleted or cancelled. FR-244 "(and the class's length where the class has one)": length per slot or per class? "Up to 14 slots" is fine. 10.3 tests only migration, not the edit rule. *Fix:* one paragraph with the three cases (move, add, remove) and a booked-session outcome.
- **medium** M6: FR-225 / "same features whatever the plan" vs attendance definition. A **Class-only** plan has "no general floor access" (FR-024) and FR-107 says class attendance is "distinct from floor check-in". If class attendance is not an "attendance record at the gym", a Class-only member has zero visit days and the ring, calendar and leaderboard are empty for them, which contradicts "All members see the same features whatever their plan". *Fix:* say whether marking class attendance creates a visit day.
- **medium** M7: FR-233 eligibility. The reminder fires on "has not visited today and a streak would end today", with no exclusion for members who cannot check in (expired, deactivated, awaiting registration fee, gym suspended) or Pay-per-session members with no usable session (the reminder would send them to a rejected scan). Nor does it say what happens at a gym that closes before 18:00. *Fix:* add an eligibility sentence; decide on the gym-hours case.
- **medium** M8: FR-234 challenge timing. "Fixed once the month starts" and "one per gym per month": can an Owner create one on the 20th (counted from the 1st? from creation)? Can they create next month's in advance? Can a reached challenge be cancelled? *Fix:* state creation window and counting start.
- **low** L6: FR-235 "can show a member a summary" is permissive, so nothing fails. "Best week" is undefined (most visit days; tie rule). FR-240 "a duration of about a year" is not testable; give the exact duration or "set by the gym". FR-229 "shows a celebration screen once": once per member across devices or per install? FR-230 "7 visits"/"30 visits"/"100 visits" badges vs "7th, 30th, 100th total visit **day**": use "visit days" everywhere (the glossary term).
- **low** L7: FR-020 does not say the phone number is "required" (FR-248 states it as "a required phone number (FR-020)", and FR-023 says changing it needs admin intervention). Cite FR-001/FR-082 or add "required" to FR-020's meaning.
