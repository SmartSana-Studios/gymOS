## Process lessons (each from the evidence)

1. **The local gate and the CI gate disagreed again, and CI first met 14 stories' pgTAP at epic end.** The first CI run on PR #122 failed `rls-tests` (run 42785046106, head `5ed9c34`; `gym_page_content.test.sql` 6 failures, `gym_page_media.test.sql` 9) because `scripts/pgtap-run.sh` accepted unnumbered `ok` lines that CI's parser rejects; `bcbdc35` fixed the files and made the runner reject them. The real `pg_prove` was not in `ci-local`, and `pgtap` ran at the same time as the `static` and `deno` stages on one machine, a "recurring false failure" seen on 24-11, 24-12, 24-13 (`deferred-work.md:3-6`; that note says pgtap and e2e, but `ci-local.sh` never ran those two together, so the wording was imprecise).
