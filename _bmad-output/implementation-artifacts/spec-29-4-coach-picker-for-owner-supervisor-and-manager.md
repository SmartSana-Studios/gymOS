## Intent

**Problem:** An Owner, Supervisor or Manager cannot check a coach's work: the Coach Portal is the coach's own, read through the caller's own RLS, and those roles have no coach read. Opening Owner and Manager to the coach's tables would also open their members' body and progress data.

**Approach:** One migration adds seven `p_coach_id` read functions (`SECURITY DEFINER`, live role gate, own gym only, named columns, no body or progress table) and the Coach Portal gets a read-only picker mode for those three roles: select, banner, Classes and Members tabs, member page with Session Notes and Workout Plan. A CI check keeps every picker function away from body and progress data. Acceptance criteria are Story 29.4 in `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md` (lines 7334-7395); UX is `EXPERIENCE.md` AD-32 (4257-4307), the picker amendments at 3560-3567, 3604, 3661, the sidebar table (378-417) and Group G (1014-1035, plus `coach.picker.backToMembers` and `.openStaff` at 1067-1068).
