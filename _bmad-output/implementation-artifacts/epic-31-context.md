# Epic 31 Context: Member Engagement Extras — Shields, Reminder, Badges, Sharing

<!-- Compiled from planning artifacts. Edit freely. Regenerate with compile-epic-context if planning docs change. -->

## Goal

Extend the Epic 30 weekly-streak core so members keep a streak through illness and come back from a break without shame: Streak Shields and a comeback state, one calm evening reminder when a visit today is what keeps the week on track, a badge shelf, and a shareable milestone image. These are Should items. The daily streak, the gym-wide monthly challenge and the "Your month" summary (all Could) are deferred to a later release and have no stories; only their hooks are kept. Production is live with real gyms and the area stays dark until the operator sets the engagement epoch.

## Stories

- Story 31.1: Streak Shields and Comeback
- Story 31.2: Streak Reminder N-08
- Story 31.3: Badge Shelf and Share Image

## Requirements & Constraints

- Shields: one is earned at every 4th consecutive met week (4, 8, 12...), held up to a cap of 2; one earned at the cap is not stored. A missed week with a Shield held is saved: the streak is kept but not increased, the Shield is spent, and a saved week does not count toward the next Shield. A missed week with none held ends the run (strict consecutive). Best streak is never lowered. After a broken run, a new Shield needs 4 more consecutive met weeks.
- Comeback: after a run ends with no Shield, 3 visit days within a 10-day window (from the first visit day after the run ended) restore active status. A lapsed window restarts on the next visit day. A met week while a comeback is open starts a new run and closes the comeback. Completing a comeback grants nothing and invents no count. Wording is calm: no failure language, no red tint.
- Shield and comeback parameters (interval 4, cap 2, comeback visits 3, window 10 days) are config columns, never hard-coded in screens.
- Corrections cascade: a late offline visit or a staff void re-folds from the earliest affected week, gives back or spends Shields, and re-evaluates later weeks. Two racing settles leave one fact and one spent Shield. Rebuild must equal the live fold on derived state (streak, best, Shields, comeback, per-week outcomes, badges).
- Nobody can buy, give, transfer or edit a Shield, streak, comeback or badge. Nothing here touches the loyalty ledger, guest sessions or stamps. No new support-capability registration.
- Reminder (N-08): at most one per member per gym-local day, sent between 18:00 and 21:00 gym-local, only for active gyms and only at or after the engagement epoch. A member is eligible when active, not opted out (on by default), subscription not expired, no counted visit today, and the weekly goal can still be met only by visiting today (visits still needed equal days left, Monday start). No plan gating, and a held Shield does not suppress it. Only the weekly condition sends; the daily-streak condition is inactive. It never blocks, locks or slows a check-in. It has one off-switch, independent of the quiet-gym and class-reminder toggles, and does not share their limits. Push opens Home, no deep link, and the title is never the gym name. Wording is bilingual and byte-pinned to the UX catalog if held inline.
- Badges: five codes, `first_visit`, `visits_7`, `visits_30`, `visits_100`, `four_weeks` (first earned Shield). A badge is earned once and never lost or revised, and its stored earned date wins over any recompute. Release back-fill is silent with real historic dates and no celebration, and `four_weeks` is never back-filled. Awarding 7, 30 or 100 is the same award as the Epic 30 milestone and never celebrates twice; `first_visit` and `four_weeks` have no celebration. Badges grant nothing.
- Share image: 1080 by 1920 PNG built on the device from local data only, handed to the OS share sheet. It contains gym name and logo, the milestone or badge, date and the member's own lit days, and never a member name, phone, payment figure, other member's data or GymOS watermark. No network call, table, endpoint, storage or analytics event records what was shared. No photo-library or storage permission is requested. The temp file is deleted after the sheet closes and on failure. It works offline. Capture failure shows a retry message; an unavailable share sheet hides the button.
- NFR: counts deterministic and recomputable; check-in and the reminder never interfere (NFR-029); the Home, calendar and leaderboard load budget is not regressed (no per-call full-history scan).

## Technical Decisions

- Extend Epic 30's fold, do not replace it: `engagement_facts` (append-only, effective fact is the highest revision, kinds `week`, `day_shield`, `milestone`, `badge`, `comeback`), `private.settle_engagement`, `rebuild_engagement`, the `engagement_state` watermark and `private.engagement_state_view`. No Shield or streak counter is stored; the number held is derived by folding effective `week` facts in order. Week outcomes are `met`, `missed` or `saved`. 31.1 and 31.3 add no table.
- `engagement_state_view` is dropped and re-created with extended columns (`shields_held`, `weeks_to_next_shield`, `shield_cap`, newest saved week, `best_streak`, `comeback` state and progress). The streak is null, not 0, when no run is active. Revoke EXECUTE from `public` and `anon` explicitly (new functions are anon-executable by default) and assert it in pgTAP.
- `day_shield` is designed but never written in V1.8. The rule "the last Shield is reserved for the weekly streak" sits in one step guarded by `private.engagement_daily_streak_enabled()` (false), with a rolled-back activation proof.
- Do not edit `check_in()`, `record_check_in` or `void_staff_check_in`; they only mark `dirty_from`. Settle runs from cron and first open, never inline in a scan.
- Reminder: a new sender `private.send_engagement_reminder(member_id)`, because `send_push_notification` only serves N-01 to N-03. Dispatch and delivery tables mirror the class-reminder shape, with a unique `(member_id, gym_local_date)` claim via `on conflict do nothing returning id`. A claim, history row, push request and delivery rows are one transaction per member; a dispatch with no deliveries is never re-sent that day. The `notifications.type` CHECK is extended to N-08 (and still rejects N-09). `private.process_notification_deliveries()` is replaced in whole, starting from the live definition at merge time, keeping every other family's behaviour. `member_preferences.streak_reminder_opted_out` defaults false. One cron job `engagement_reminder` every 15 minutes through a `run_engagement_reminder_job()` function (EXECUTE revoked), logging one `job_runs` row per run. The job reads only the at-risk flag, visits still needed and days left from `engagement_state_view`, reads `attendance_counted`, takes no member lock and never writes facts. N-07 text is inline in its sender today; Epic 30's wording store is used if server-readable, otherwise inline with a byte-for-byte test.
- Badges: `private.award_milestones` (Epic 30) is extended to award all five badges under the member lock, idempotent via the unique key, `earned_on` stored in the payload. `get_my_badges()` is a member-owned SECURITY DEFINER read that never awards, returns exactly five rows with progress, and gives only the caller's own data.
- Share library decision is made in 31.3 on the day of the work, verified on the web against Expo SDK 57. The default is `react-native-view-shot` at the SDK-bundled version plus `expo-sharing`; the fallback is the already-installed `react-native-svg` and `expo-file-system`. Record it as a dated top entry in `docs/decisions.md` and update the spine's deferred bullet. Both candidates add native code, so the feature ships only in a new EAS store build (no OTA channel); state the build order and do not run the build.
- Wording lives in `apps/mobile/src/locales/{en,fr}.json`. Epic 30.1 already added the Shield, comeback, reminder and celebration keys, so 31.1 and 31.2 verify and use them; 31.3 adds `engage.badges.*` and `engage.share.*`. Reserved mascot keys stay empty. `scripts/check-i18n-key-parity.mjs` must pass. Shield strings never use "freeze", "insurance" or "token" (FR uses "bouclier").
- Testing: no device E2E harness. Coverage is pgTAP (including a two-session race script) plus mobile component tests. Real-library 1080 by 1920 output and the WhatsApp hand-off are checked by the product owner on a device build. Migration numbers are assigned at merge time, and the existing pgTAP suite passes without fixture edits except the Epic 30 tests each story names.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

- Home "This week" card (Epic 30) gains a Shield chip (only when 1 or 2 held, never "0 Shields", 44pt target, opens an explainer sheet), a muted next-Shield or max-Shields line, dismissible "Shield saved" and "Shield earned" notice rows (one at a time, newest first, dismissal persisted on the device), and an optional "Shield is ready" second line in the at-risk hint.
- Comeback card phases: active with no visit (three empty stamp segments, no "0 / 3"), 1 or 2 visits, window lapsed (restart wording, no warning), restored (one-time message, then the chip reads "Best: n weeks" until a new week is met). Numbers come from the server.
- A queued offline visit shows only as the dashed pending segment and never changes a Shield or comeback figure locally. The calendar gains a Shield legend entry and a week-saved glyph.
- Profile Notifications card gains a "Streak reminder" toggle, on by default, saved immediately with an optimistic flip and revert on error, helper text with the send time ("6 pm" EN, "18 h" FR) read from the job's config. Notification history renders N-08 with a primary-tinted icon.
- Badge Shelf (`/badges`, MA-21) is opened from a Profile "Badges" row ("2 of 5") or from the celebration's "See your badges" link (which acknowledges and replaces the route). It is a two-column grid of five tiles in fixed order. Locked tiles show progress, never a lock icon alone and never "0 of N" as failure. A tapped earned tile opens a detail sheet with Share; a locked tile opens it without Share. It shows skeletons, an error card, cached offline tiles, and pull to refresh.
- Celebration (MA-20) gains Share as the primary button, "Keep going" secondary and "See your badges" as a text link. Share shows a spinner without changing width; iPad anchors the sheet to the button. Cancelled or successful share shows nothing. The Epic 30 behaviour (acknowledge, reduced motion, offline) is unchanged.
- Tone: calm and never guilt-based. Shape or glyph always accompanies colour, and layouts must hold at reduced text size.

## Cross-Story Dependencies

- Hard dependency on Epic 30: the fold and settle, the state view, `member_visit_days` and `attendance_counted`, the milestone award path, the calendar, the Home card and the wording catalog. Also on Epic 27 attendance (`record_check_in`, void). The Home "This week" card and the celebration screen are modified, so their existing tests are touched only where a story names them.
- 31.1 and 31.2 are independent of each other once Epic 30 is merged. 31.2 reads the state view's at-risk flags and `platform_engagement_config.release_week_start`.
- 31.3 depends on 31.1: the `four_weeks` badge is awarded from the fold's first earned Shield. It also extends Epic 30's award step and its celebration screen.
- Live notification paths change in 31.2 (`process_notification_deliveries`, the `notifications.type` CHECK), so the existing notification pgTAP files must pass unedited except `notification_history.test.sql`, which pins the CHECK list. Run the full `scripts/ci-local.sh` gate for each story.
- Deferred Could items (daily streak, gym challenge, "Your month") keep their hooks: the `day_shield` kind and its guard function, the `n08.daily.*` and `engage.atRisk.daily` keys, and the reminder's single extra flag. Nothing in this epic implements them.
