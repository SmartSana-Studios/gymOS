Who does what

Six roles. A person's role decides which sections exist for them — hidden sections are blocked in the database too, not just in the menu.

Super AdminPlatform staff. Creates gyms and tiers, sets pricing, manages the payment and messaging providers. Not a member of any gym.
OwnerRuns one gym end to end, including staff and settings. The only role that can appoint a Supervisor.
SupervisorDeputy to the Owner: staff, settings and classes. Deliberately has no access to members, money or the audit log.
ManagerDay-to-day operations: members, plans, subscriptions, payments, attendance, classes and the audit log.
ReceptionistFront desk: check members in, take payments, look people up. No pricing or staff control.
CoachSees only the Coach Portal — assigned members, their plans and session notes.
MemberUses the mobile app only. No dashboard access of any kind.

Super Admin manual

Seven sections, in the order you will typically need them.

GymsCreate a gym with its owner's details and starting tier, then browse and filter by status. Open a gym to change its tier, suspend it, or reinstate it — suspension and reinstatement both require a typed justification, and a suspended gym's staff see a lock screen instead of the dashboard.
TiersCreate and edit the subscription tiers gyms are sold on: monthly price, annual price and member cap. A tier that gyms already sit on can be price-locked so existing customers keep their agreed rate.
BillingEach gym's SaaS billing state — paid, due, or past due — and the payments they have made for their own subscription. A past-due gym gets a Pay Now prompt inside its dashboard.
MetricsPlatform-wide totals across all gyms.
AdminsAdd another Super Admin by email, or promote an existing account. Every change is attributed to the person who made it in the audit log.
MessagingHolds the WhatsApp sending instance used for both staff invites and member sign-in codes. The field is labelled Instance ID but expects the instance name, matched exactly as it appears in the messaging provider — including spaces and capitalisation. A mismatch silently stops both invites and sign-in codes.
Payment ProvidersWhich mobile-money provider is active platform-wide. Individual gyms then connect their own account under their dashboard settings.

Member manual

The mobile app, on iOS and Android.

Getting in

Sign inChoose a language, pick your country and enter your phone number, then type the code sent to you. There is no password — the code is how you sign in every time. Too many wrong attempts locks the screen briefly.
Set upAdd your name and photo, optionally your body measurements, then your goal and experience level. Finally confirm your plan. You are only fully signed up once you reach the main tabs.

Day to day

HomeYour membership status, when it expires, and what is coming up.
Check inScan the QR code at the gym entrance. It works without a signal — scans are queued and sent when you are back online.
ClassesBrowse the timetable and book a place. You get a reminder before the session.
ProgressLog weight and measurements over time, and keep progress photos. Photos are private unless you choose to share them with your coach.
HistoryYour past visits and payments, with a receipt for each one.
Workout planThe plan your coach set for you, with exercises to tick off as you complete them.
RenewRenew when your membership is running out. Pay by mobile money from your phone — pick your country, enter the number, and approve the prompt on your handset.
ProfileYour details, language and notification preferences. Your phone number is your sign-in identity, so the gym changes it for you rather than you editing it here.

Before you demo

Live moneyMobile money is configured with production credentials. A payment started here is a real transaction — do not run the payment flow as a demo step.
Empty startThe environment was reset on 8 September 2026. It holds one Super Admin and one gym, with no historical member, payment or attendance data to browse.
Sign-in codesCodes go out over WhatsApp first and fall back to SMS. The store-review number skips delivery entirely and always accepts its fixed code.
SupersededThe July 2026 version of this page said Members, Subscriptions, Payments and Attendance were navigation-only. That is no longer true — those modules are built, and Coach Portal is role-gated rather than hidden.
